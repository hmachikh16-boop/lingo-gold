## Full description (≥ 50 words)
Lin Go is an Arabic-language app for learning English and French through a complete path of 740 lessons across 4 stages (A1–B1), with 58 stories, cartoon dialogues, voice pronunciation (Arabic/English/French), vocabulary flashcards (SRS), quizzes, games, and challenges. It includes an AI Tutor with an offline fallback, a daily progress path, and encrypted password-protected backups. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. Suitable for all levels (beginner → advanced) with a placement assessment. The UI supports Arabic, English, and French. Lesson/story/quiz content updates are downloaded inside the app (DownloadManager) without opening a browser.
